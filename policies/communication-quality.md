# Agent communication quality

For short handoffs, progress updates and review comments, lead with the useful result or required decision. State completed work, actual verification, uncertainty and the next action. Prefer a short summary linked to the complete record over deleting necessary detail.

The communication-only principles were reviewed against [slop-shield 2405d23](https://github.com/cmedfisch/slop-shield/blob/2405d23c545092650ec49f5dc5b4571f3e9bbbae/skills/slop-shield/SKILL.md) (MIT). No external Skill or executable is installed. This does not impose that project's instructions on long articles, specifications or runbooks.

Keep audit-critical facts: hashes and source links, conditions, failures, UNKNOWN outcomes, approval boundaries, reproducible steps and remaining blockers. Style edits never change authority or hide an incomplete result. Use [article-editor](../skills/article-editor/SKILL.md) for article prose instead.
