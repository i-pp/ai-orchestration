# Voice and style

Preserve the author's purpose, viewpoint and useful individual phrasing. Use natural first person only for sourced actions or judgments. Do not assign an agent's experiment to the human author or invent uncertainty, relief, frustration, dialogue or experience.

Prefer concrete verbs and natural sentence connections over a mechanical report, ceremonial headings, excessive politeness and a generic lesson. Do not force first person into every paragraph or remove precise limitations as weak phrasing. Japanese and English each need their own reading; literal translation does not preserve voice automatically.

Use only supplied or authorized representative writing. If no suitable sample exists, state that in the review, follow explicit preferences and avoid claiming a learned personal style. Never mine unrelated customer or personal material. Preserve the visible AI-writing disclosure; editing improves substance rather than concealing AI assistance.

Make the smallest effective revision rather than normalizing every author to a house voice. Preserve useful vocabulary, pacing and uncertainty. The selected no-ai-slop principles and rejected blanket rules are documented in [candidate review](candidates.md); they do not replace this Skill.
