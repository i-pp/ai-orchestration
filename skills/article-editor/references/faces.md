Apply [v2.0](../../../docs/ai/editorial-policy.md). Acceptance must be real, not generated by a checker. It may be the resolved technical/editorial review disposition; it is not an added requirement for human approval of every edit. Do not overwrite frozen B; changes require a new accepted revision.

Record A separately with its file hash, optionally timezone-aware `a_created_at` no earlier than `b_frozen_at` (equal recorded seconds are allowed), `face_a_source_b_sha256` equal to the frozen B hash and actual editor identity. Preserve the unified diff. List substantive changes (claims, values, commands, uncertainty or attribution), with technical disposition and any factual disagreement. Cosmetic edits to A do not require refreezing B or a new human approval. They need no invented substantive finding. Resolve substantive disagreement or escalate; keep ordinary revision within two cycles. Give separate technical and editorial conclusions; answer all six v2 acceptance questions before proposing release. Neither a hash nor lint success supplies those judgments.

It does not run ChatGPT, freeze file permissions, prove an acceptance claim or classify meaning automatically. Keep its output in private supporting records, not in the article opening.
