## Workflow

1. **Evidence.** Identify the author, reader, problem, source topic IDs and evidence hashes. Separate observations, reports, hypotheses and opinions. Use authorized material only; do not collect unrelated private writing. Note which author's actions can support first person. Preserve raw evidence.
2. **Topic fitness and structure.** Before drafting, state the reader's concrete question, the evidence for resolution/non-resolution, reproducible steps and the decision they support, and what this adds beyond existing primary documentation. Choose LONG_FORM, SHORT_NOTE or DEFER; do not inflate a weak topic. Decide what the reader can learn from this particular investigation. Trace the supported chain from tools/desired work through failure, unsuccessful attempt, diagnosis, change, result and a concrete reader takeaway. Identify the assigned task/model when AI evaluation is the subject; this is not a requirement for unrelated engineering investigations. Distinguish what the records show happened when from the order used to explain it; never fabricate a later discovery or successful result. Keep this a narrative, not a fixed list of section headings. Sketch a short path from situation and motivation through the obstacle, discoveries, attempted responses and remaining uncertainty. Adapt the structure to the evidence rather than filling a template.
3. **Draft.** Write the title and opening for a search reader with no project knowledge: lead with the desired task, obstacle/decision, bounded comparison, relevant model/tool/language and the finding or usable information. Put the concrete bug or example after this purpose, and avoid a long project introduction or an invented motivation. Let a Writer produce the intended-language narrative. Ground the author's voice in actual supplied or authorized writing examples; if no representative published prose is available, say so in the review record and follow the user's stated preferences. Never turn an agent's experiment into the author's personal act.
   For substantial investigations, give busy readers the purpose, success goal, actual result and useful next action early, with only the limitations that change their decision. Label a launcher for an already-built environment as Working Configuration or Troubleshooting; Quick Start requires the complete starting environment, acquisition/setup/build/run path, success criteria and relevant recovery. Do not fabricate missing steps. Consolidate repeated audit caveats in supporting evidence and a focused limitations section while retaining material qualifications beside the affected claim.
4. **Mechanical lint.** Use [article-editor](../article-editor/SKILL.md) and its pinned local checker: textlint for Japanese, Vale for English, optional local LanguageTool. For Japanese, invoke the approved noslop adapter only on an explicitly authorized sanitized copy with its exact SHA256; see article-editor. Keep actual outcomes, versions and hashes; no unpublished draft API upload or silent auto-fix.
5. **Editorial, then technical review.** The independent Editor assesses purpose, reader value, reasoning and supported author voice using article-editor. The separate Technical Reviewer verifies claims, values, commands, source attribution and limits. Do not substitute test counts for either judgment.
6. **Targeted revision and final verification.** Use the severity/disposition and two-cycle limit in article-editor. Preserve raw/edited/diff and review protected spans against evidence. Resolve BLOCKER findings; document a specific rationale for accepted MAJOR findings. MINOR/SUGGESTION findings and unavailable optional tooling do not trigger endless review. Unverified results stay unverified; changed article bytes invalidate final review.
7. **Publication preparation.** Add a visible opening disclosure of ChatGPT-assisted writing and an actual platform metadata tag/topic; name other material AI assistance accurately. Do not treat a body hashtag as platform metadata. Verify this separately from quality. Use the medium's skill to validate title/body rendering, frontmatter, links, preview, dedicated branch and draft PR. Report technical and editorial findings separately, exact artifact/commit identity, unresolved items and requested decisions. Require explicit human approval before merge or publication.

Reuse the existing Codex/Local LLM roles with bounded assignments as Writer, Editor and Technical Reviewer. Do not install a new agent configuration merely to label these responsibilities. When delegation is unavailable, record a self-review as such and keep independent review pending.
