## Workflow

1. **Evidence.** Identify the author, reader, problem, source topic IDs and evidence hashes. Separate observations, reports, hypotheses and opinions. Use authorized material only; do not collect unrelated private writing. Note which author's actions can support first person. Preserve raw evidence.
2. **Topic fitness and structure.** Before drafting, state the reader's concrete question, the evidence for resolution/non-resolution, reproducible steps and the decision they support, and what this adds beyond existing primary documentation. Choose LONG_FORM, SHORT_NOTE or DEFER; do not inflate a weak topic. Decide what the reader can learn from this particular investigation. Trace the supported chain from tools/desired work and the task assigned to an identified AI through failure, unsuccessful attempt, diagnosis, change, result and a concrete reader takeaway. Distinguish what the records show happened when from the order used to explain it; never fabricate a later discovery or successful result. Keep this a narrative, not a fixed list of section headings. Sketch a short path from situation and motivation through the obstacle, discoveries, attempted responses and remaining uncertainty. Adapt the structure to the evidence rather than filling a template.
3. **Draft.** Write the title and opening for a search reader with no project knowledge: lead with the desired task, obstacle/decision, bounded comparison, relevant model/tool/language and the finding or usable information. Put the concrete bug or example after this purpose, and avoid a long project introduction or an invented motivation. Let a Writer produce the intended-language narrative. Ground the author's voice in actual supplied or authorized writing examples; if no representative published prose is available, say so in the review record and follow the user's stated preferences. Never turn an agent's experiment into the author's personal act.
4. **Technical verification.** Give a separate Technical Reviewer the draft, source references and commands. Check attribution, values, scope, reproducibility, failure explanations and unsupported causal claims. Keep later experiments separately sourced and dated. Resolve factual blockers before preparing delivery.
5. **Editing.** Have an Editor read it as a reader: is the opening understandable, the investigation easy to follow and the voice natural? Remove report-like framing, stock transitions and needless repetition without deleting material uncertainty. Independently check the title and opening alone for an unfamiliar, goal-oriented reader: target, task, problem, comparison, result and limits must be intelligible without project context. Then answer “So what can the reader now do or decide?” and check the promised answer against evidence. A natural sequence/first person is not acceptance. Return concrete before/after examples and reasons, not only lint scores. Reopen acceptance when the user rejects reader value.
6. **Polish.** Re-read the whole article in its publication language. Preserve raw/edited versions and a diff; flag number, quote, source and limitation changes for human review. Record tool names, pinned versions, commands and actual outcomes. Unavailable tools stay NOT_RUN; human editing does not imply tool execution or waive an existing gate.
7. **Publication preparation.** Add a visible opening disclosure of ChatGPT-assisted writing and an actual platform metadata tag/topic; name other material AI assistance accurately. Do not treat a body hashtag as platform metadata. Verify this separately from quality. Use the medium's skill to validate title/body rendering, frontmatter, links, preview, dedicated branch and draft PR. Report technical and editorial findings separately, exact artifact/commit identity, unresolved items and requested decisions. Require explicit human approval before merge or publication.

Reuse the existing Codex/Local LLM roles with bounded assignments as Writer, Editor and Technical Reviewer. Do not install a new agent configuration merely to label these responsibilities. When delegation is unavailable, record a self-review as such and keep independent review pending.

## Handoff

Provide the draft and diff, source/claim map, evidence for first-person passages, independent editorial and technical findings, preview/validation results, and remaining delivery holds. Keep sensitive raw logs and operational credentials outside Git. Do not claim that a skill is globally installed merely because this repository contains its source.
